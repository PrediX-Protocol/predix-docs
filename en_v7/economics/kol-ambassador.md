# KOL & Ambassador program

Dedicated pool of **12M PRX** from S1 (86M). Milestone-based — tokens are only distributed when milestones are met, not time-based.

## 2 tracks

| Track | Entry | Tiers | Max PRX | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Track 1 — Open Funnel** | >= 1K followers, ER >= 5% | Trial -> Active -> Qualified | 8,000 PRX | Ambassadors, micro-influencers |
| **Track 2 — Direct Entry** | >= 2K followers or Team invite | Bronze -> Silver -> Gold | 300,000 PRX | KOLs, macro-influencers |

## Track 1 — Open Funnel

Anyone can participate with >= 1K followers + engagement rate >= 5%. Requires linking an X/Twitter account with a wallet (OAuth).

### Tier 1: Trial — 1,000 PRX

| Milestone | Requirement | PRX | Deadline | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Post 5 pieces of content about PrediX (quality check) | 200 | 14 days | 200 |
| T2 | 3 referrals complete a trade >= $10 | 300 | 14 days | 500 |
| T3 | Active continuously for 14 days | 500 | 14 days | 1,000 |

Failure to complete within 14 days = auto-drop. Tokens are not distributed — they remain in the pool.

### Tier 2: Active — 2,000 PRX (total 3,000)

Entry: complete Trial. Auto-qualify.

| Milestone | Requirement | PRX | Deadline | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 5 referrals complete their first trade | 500 | 30 days | 1,500 |
| A2 | 10 posts/month + 1 video or thread | 500 | 30 days | 2,000 |
| A3 | Total 10 refs + ref volume $5K | 500 | 60 days | 2,500 |
| A4 | Maintain continuous content for 2 months | 500 | 60 days | 3,000 |

Failure to complete = freeze. Allocation from Trial is retained.

### Tier 3: Qualified — 5,000 PRX (total 8,000)

Entry: complete Active + (>= 2K followers OR ER >= 5%).

| Milestone | Requirement | PRX | Deadline | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 20 referrals + ref volume $10K | 1,500 | 30 days | 4,500 |
| Q2 | 12 posts + 2 videos + 1 Space/AMA live | 1,500 | 30 days | 6,000 |
| Q3 | Top 30% performance + maintain for 30 days | 2,000 | 30 days | 8,000 |

Completing Q3 -> eligible for Direct Entry Bronze (Track 2).

**Total Track 1**: 10 milestones, ~5-6 months, up to 8,000 PRX.

## Vesting

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **TGE unlock** | 20% at TGE |
| **Linear vesting** | Remaining 80%: linear over 6 months (daily release) |
| **Upgrade delta** | PRX earned upon tier upgrade has its own vesting, starting from upgrade date |
| **Freeze on downgrade** | Already unlocked tokens are retained. Unvested tokens are frozen. |
| **Clawback** | Inactive >= 6 months -> unvested PRX returned to pool |

## How to participate

1. Ensure you meet the follower requirement (>= 1K for Track 1, >= 2K for Direct Bronze).
2. Link your X/Twitter account with your PrediX wallet (OAuth binding).
3. Begin milestones — tokens activate when milestones are verified.
4. Track progress in the **KOL Dashboard** on the app.

Contact: [business@predix.app](mailto:business@predix.app) for Direct Entry or team invite.
