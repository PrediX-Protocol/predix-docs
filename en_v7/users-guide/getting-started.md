# Quick start

### First Trade

| 🔐 **Setup** (\~10s)         | 💰 **Funding** (\~30s)         | 📈 **Trade** (\~20s)   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Open app                     | Bridge USDC from another chain | Browse markets         |
| Sign in (passkey / MetaMask) | Or deposit directly from a CEX | Buy YES or NO          |
|                              |                                | Track P\&L in realtime |

## 3 Steps

1. [**Connect wallet**](first-trade/connect-wallet.md) — Passkey (biometric, web2 UX) or crypto wallet (MetaMask / Rainbow / WalletConnect). Eligible users may receive gas sponsorship via paymaster.
2. [**Bridge USDC**](first-trade/bridge.md) — If your USDC is on another chain, bridge it to Unichain first.
3. [**First trade**](first-trade/) — Buy YES or NO in 30 seconds.

**Earn PRX now**: Every action on PrediX earns [Points](../economics/points.md) — converting to PRX tokens at TGE. FIFA WC boost 2.5-3x during M5-M6.

Track your positions in realtime at [Portfolio](first-trade/portfolio.md).
