# Getting Started

Make your first trade on PrediX in under 2 minutes.

## Overview

| 🔐 **Setup** (~10s) | 💰 **Funding** (~30s) | 📈 **Trade** (~20s) |
|---|---|---|
| Open app | Bridge USDC from another chain | Browse markets |
| Sign in (passkey / MetaMask) | Or deposit directly from a CEX | Buy YES or NO |
| | | Track P&L in realtime |

## 3 Steps

1. **[Connect wallet](connect-wallet.md)** — Passkey (biometric, web2 UX) or crypto wallet (MetaMask / Rainbow / WalletConnect). Eligible users may receive gas sponsorship via paymaster.
2. **[Bridge USDC](bridge.md)** — If your USDC is on another chain, bridge it to Unichain first.
3. **[First trade](first-trade.md)** — Buy YES or NO in 30 seconds.

**Earn PRX now**: Every action on PrediX earns [Points](points.md) — converting to PRX tokens at TGE. FIFA WC boost 2.5-3x during M5-M6.

Track your positions in realtime at [Portfolio](../guides/portfolio.md).
